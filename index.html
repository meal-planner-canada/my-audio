Add an episode or song that you own — paste an audio link or pick the file from your phone — then add a picture for its cover.

How adding works:
1. Tap Add and name the episode.
2. Choose Link and paste a direct audio link (ends in .mp3, .m4a…), or choose File and pick the audio off your phone.
3. Add a cover: pick a picture from your photos, paste an image link, or use a colour tile.
4. Hit Add to library and press play.
Listening on the lock screen

Press play, then lock your phone or switch apps — playback keeps going and your lock screen / Control Centre shows the title, show and cover with play, pause, skip and next / previous controls. For the most reliable background play on iPhone, open this in Safari or from your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and start it there. Inside another app's preview, iOS can stop web audio when you leave — and if you force-close Safari, playback stops, because a web page has no background service running when it's closed.

Your library is saved in this browser on this phone — links, files you pick, covers, favourites and where you left off. Keep using it in the same browser you added it in; clearing that browser's site data will clear the library too. Links still play from wherever they're hosted — a direct audio link works best.
